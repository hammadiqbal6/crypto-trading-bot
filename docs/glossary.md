# Glossary

- **TSMOM** — Time-series momentum: trade each asset on its own trend.
- **Cross-sectional momentum** — Rank assets vs peers; overweight winners.
- **Vol targeting** — Scale position inversely to realised volatility.
- **DSR** — Deflated Sharpe Ratio (Bailey & López de Prado).
- **PBO** — Probability of Backtest Overfitting.
- **Dry run** — Simulated fills inside freqtrade (no real orders).
- **Demo trading** — Exchange-provided fake funds (Bybit Demo).
- **Holdout** — Untouched final sample for evaluation only.
